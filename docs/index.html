The Mancer's echo

A fantasy card duel game, small tribute to Spectromancer©.

About

The unity of the elements seems to have been replaced by an order you no longer understand. Unsure whether the cause lies within you, you set off on a journey—a voyage to a fantastical land where you will encounter mages of various persuasions.

Download Grimoire

The game is an AI port of a personal project with some improvements (fixes, enhancements) and generic AI generated art in dark fantasy MTG style.

The campaign is supposed to happen a long time after the epoch of the original game. You would better play the original game first to understand the game mechanics.

Houses from community suggestions

Vampires, Shadowmage, Entomologist and more, each with its own creatures and spells.

Campaign & duels

Work through the campaign, or pick a duel against a bot.

Internationalization

Contains AI translations in multiple languages (English, French, Spanish, Russian, Vietnamese).

Credits

You can support the original game by buying it on Spectromancer.com. The art is great (music and graphics). There are tutorials, a beginner friendly campaign, multiplayer mode, and the classes are well balanced and archetypal.

Thanks to the Spectromancer community for sharing their class suggestions destinated to the original game, and to the AI tools that make this project possible. I excuse in advance if I misused any of their ideas.

Limitations:

  • Not all rules of the original game are implemented (the 1% rule, some deck rules).
  • There are still some bugs.
  • Hidden knowledge is not completely hidden for some bots (stealth cards, or meteomancer called element with mistwalker for example).
  • The classes are not well balanced. In the campaign, you may have a hard time to beat paganist, highpriest for example. (Seasons is also quite strong, but in the campaign the bot used is weaker.)

Tips: For the beginners, I think the classes that are easier to play: Shaman, Troopers (a rebranding of the original mecha by a starcraft fan I think). Some are not easy but can be very effective once understood: Antimancer, Conjurer for example. You can adapt the difficulty by giving the AI more or less time to think.

Class updates:
